Our work

Three programs, one aim: resilient livelihoods.

Everything we do sits under one of these three programs — the first is what we deliver, the second is how we know it is working, the third is how we make sure it was never ours alone to decide.

Livelihoods & Food Systems

Strengthens household and community food systems through co-created, data-informed interventions that improve productivity, income, and resilience.

  • Sustainable and climate-smart production practices
  • Natural resource governance and stewardship
  • Market access and financial inclusion for smallholders and local entrepreneurs
  • Youth engagement in food systems livelihoods

Data, Evidence & Adaptive Management

Builds the evidence base that drives every program decision, blending quantitative and participatory methods.

  • Mobile surveys and geospatial mapping for remote and hard-to-reach communities
  • Participatory qualitative methods: interviews, focus groups, and case studies
  • Real-time feedback loops for adaptive program adjustment
  • Impact measurement and learning products for communities, partners, and funders

Community Co-Creation & Partnerships

Ensures every solution is designed with, not for, the communities it serves.

  • Participatory design and planning workshops
  • Local leadership and capacity building
  • Partnerships with governments, local innovators, and community institutions
  • Community involvement in monitoring, evaluation, and decision-making
